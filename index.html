Methodology

How to read
the findings.

These results describe historical transactions in the DataCo Smart Supply Chain dataset. The repository includes the profiling notebook, model and report.

Coverage
Orders span January 2015 to January 2018. The project flags January 2018 as partial for monthly comparisons.
Grain and exclusions
Each row represents an order item. Cleaning excluded identifying fields, sparsely populated fields and a constant product-status field.
Financial measures
Sales and profit use recorded dataset units. Reported profit comes from the source field and lacks independent audit verification.
Delivery measures
The risk indicator and recorded delivery status are separate fields. The executive SQL exposes late-delivery rates with and without canceled shipping in the denominator.
Reproduction
The raw and processed CSVs are excluded from the public repository. Reproduction requires the authorized dataset and a local PostgreSQL connection.
Validation priorities
Review geographic coordinates, remaining missing values and business metric definitions before applying this workflow to new data.
